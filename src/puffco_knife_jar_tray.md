# Puffco Knife + Jar Tray — Assembly Notes

Holds a Puffco Hot Knife (model 041879) and two 42×42 mm square jars (with lids).
Prints as two parts: **tray** and **lid**.

## Print settings

| Setting | Recommendation |
|---|---|
| Layer height | 0.2 mm |
| Infill | 20 % |
| Supports | None required |
| Orientation | Flat (as designed) |

The lid prints upside-down (open face up) to avoid supports inside the recesses.

## Magnet hardware

**4× neodymium disc — 6 mm diameter × 3 mm thick (N35 or stronger)**

Available from most hardware stores or online (search "6x3 neodymium disc magnet").
N52 grade gives the strongest hold but N35–N52 all work.

## Magnet installation

The tray has **4 pockets on its top face** and the lid has **4 matching pockets on its bottom face**. Magnets press-fit into all 8 pockets.

```
Tray top face (looking down):

  ┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
  │  ●        [  jar 1  ]  gap  [  jar 2  ]        ●   │  ← magnets at
  │  ●                                             ●   │    front & rear
  │           [      knife slot                   ]    │    of each side
  └──────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### Step 1 — test polarity before gluing

Drop one magnet into each tray pocket (don't glue yet). Place the lid on the tray. Each lid pocket should sit directly above a tray pocket. Drop a magnet into each lid pocket and let it self-orient — it will flip to the attracting pole automatically if dropped in loosely.

Once all 8 magnets are in and the lid snaps closed correctly, mark the top face of each lid magnet with a marker before removing them. Reinstall with marked face up to preserve polarity.

### Step 2 — secure with adhesive

Use a small drop of **CA glue (super glue)** or **epoxy** in each pocket before pressing the magnet flush. Wipe any squeeze-out immediately. Allow to cure fully before use.

> **Tip:** if two adjacent magnets repel each other in a pocket, flip one of them over. Polarity only matters between the tray magnet and its matching lid magnet — not between neighboring magnets in the same part.

## Lid geometry

The lid is a **box cover** (23 mm tall) that fits over the loaded tray. The inside has shaped recesses so it sits flat against the tray rim without pressing on the items:

| Recess | Depth | Clears |
|---|---|---|
| Jar slots (×2) | 20 mm | 18 mm protrusion + 2 mm |
| Knife slot | 8 mm | 6 mm protrusion + 2 mm |

Orient the lid so its recesses align with the tray contents before closing.
