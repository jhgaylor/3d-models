# Puffco Jar + Knife Caddy — Assembly Notes

A standalone desk caddy (**not** part of the modular tray system). It holds two
42×42 mm jars and a Puffco Hot Knife (model 041879) under a **flip-top lid**, with
a pull-out **drawer** underneath for cotton swabs / qtips.

Prints as four parts: **carcass**, **deck**, **drawer**, **lid** — plus a hinge
pin and four magnets.

```
            ┌─────────────────────────┐
   lid  ──► │   flip-top (hinged rear) │
            ├─────────────────────────┤
   deck ──► │  [ jar ] [ jar ]  knife  │  ← drops into the carcass
            ├─────────────────────────┤
 drawer ──► │   qtips  (slides out) ▭  │  ← pull from the front
            └─────────────────────────┘
```

## Parts

| Part | What it is |
|---|---|
| `carcass` | The body. Open top, open front. Rear hinge knuckles. The drawer slides in the front; the deck drops in the top and rests on two internal side shelves. |
| `deck` | The jar + knife tray. Seats in the carcass, flush with the rim. Two jar pockets at the front, a full-width knife channel at the rear, magnet pockets in the side margins. |
| `drawer` | A qtip tray with a full-width front flange and finger scallop. |
| `lid` | A flip-top cover. Hinges to the carcass on a 3 mm pin; internal recesses nest over the protruding jars/knife; magnet-latched at the front. |

## Print settings

| Setting | Recommendation |
|---|---|
| Layer height | 0.2 mm |
| Infill | 20 % |
| Supports | **None required** |
| Orientation | `carcass`, `deck`, `drawer` as-modelled (flat). Print `lid` **upside-down** (open face up) so the recesses need no support. |

The carcass is open-topped, so there are no internal bridges. The hinge bores run
horizontally — push the pin through to clear any minor first-layer sag (ream with a
3 mm bit if needed).

## Hardware

**Hinge pin:** 1× **3 mm steel rod** or a length of 3 mm filament, ~125 mm long
(bore is 3.2 mm). Trim flush after fitting.

**Magnets:** 4× **neodymium disc, 6 mm ø × 3 mm thick** (N35 or stronger). Two go
in the deck (pockets open up) and two in the lid (pockets open down); they meet on
the parting line when the lid is closed.

## Assembly

1. **Hinge.** Set the lid against the back of the carcass so the knuckles interleave,
   then slide the 3 mm pin through the barrel. Trim the pin flush.
2. **Magnets.** Drop a magnet into each deck pocket (don't glue yet). Close the lid;
   drop a magnet into each lid pocket and let it self-orient to the attracting pole.
   Mark polarity, then secure all four with a dab of CA glue or epoxy, flush to the
   surface. Let cure before use.
3. **Deck.** Lower the deck into the carcass — it rests on the two side shelves,
   flush with the rim.
4. **Drawer.** Slide it into the front opening. The flange stops it; the deck above
   keeps it from lifting.

> Polarity only matters between each deck magnet and the lid magnet above it — not
> between neighbours. If two repel in adjacent pockets, flip one.

|---|---|---|---|

\* including the hinge barrel projecting ~6 mm behind the back wall.

> **Carry note:** like the rest of the kit, this is an upright caddy — the drawer is
> gravity-retained and will slide if tipped forward. Carry it level.
